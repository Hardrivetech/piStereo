BOM — Shopping list with recommended parts and vendor search terms

Core SBC
- Raspberry Pi 5 Model B (4GB or 8GB) — vendors: Raspberry Pi official, Digi-Key, Mouser, Adafruit, The Pi Hut, Amazon. Search: "Raspberry Pi 5 Model B".
- microSD (high-endurance) 32–128 GB — examples: "Samsung PRO Endurance 64GB" or "SanDisk High Endurance".

Audio (recommended paths)
- Option A (recommended, easiest multi‑RCA outputs): USB multichannel audio interface (4‑output) — example: "Behringer UMC404HD (USB 4‑in/4‑out)". Note: outputs may be TRS — get TRS→RCA adapters/cables.
- Option B (HAT/I2S multichannel): "AudioInjector Octo" or similar multichannel HAT (verify Pi 5 driver compatibility before purchase).
- RCA panel jacks — search: "panel mount RCA jack" (3 stereo pairs: Front, Rear, Sub).
- Shielded RCA cables & TRS→RCA adapters.

Amplification & speaker outputs
- 4‑channel car amplifier (RCA inputs → speaker outputs). Search: "4 channel car amplifier board TDA7850" or branded compact 4ch car amps from Alpine/Rockford/etc. Choose RMS ratings suitable for your speakers.
- Speaker screw terminal blocks (8‑position) for the rear of the unit.

Power & protection
- Automotive DC→DC buck converter 12V→5V, 6–10 A (recommended 8–10 A for headroom). Search: "12V to 5V 10A buck converter automotive".
- Car power controller / auto shutoff module (recommended) — search: "car PC power controller auto power off" or "Raspberry Pi car power module".
- Inline fuse holder + fuse (10A) — fuse near battery/source.
- TVS transient suppressor (e.g., SMBJ series) for VIN (optional but recommended for older vehicles).

Controls & connectors
- Rotary encoder with push (detent) — search: "EC11 rotary encoder with push" (panel mount option recommended).
- Momentary pushbuttons for prev/next/play/power — search: "12mm panel momentary switch" or generic PCB momentary switches.
- Molex/K.K. connector housings or screw terminal blocks for power and signal harness.

Mechanical & mounting
- Universal double‑DIN mounting kit and bezel — search: "double DIN universal mounting kit".
- Panel‑mount RCA and speaker terminal panels.
- Chassis: universal double‑DIN metal pocket or custom 3D printed faceplate.

Cables & wiring
- Shielded RCA cable set (3 pairs) for preouts.
- TRS→RCA cables/adapters (if USB interface has TRS outs).
- Speaker wire 14–16 AWG.
- Power wire 14–12 AWG for amplifier, 18–16 AWG for Pi feed.
- Ring terminals, heat shrink, zip ties, solderless butt connectors/crimps.

Accessories & misc
- Ground loop isolator (if hum occurs).
- Heatsink/fan for Pi and audio interface if enclosed.
- Panel labels, wire ID tags, mounting screws.

Notes & guidance
- Vendor suggestions: Digi-Key / Mouser for connectors, Adafruit for Pi accessories, Amazon/Thomann/Sweetwater for audio interfaces, local car audio shops for amplifiers and speaker terminals.
- For the USB multichannel interface, confirm the output connector type (TRS vs RCA) and buy adapters/cable accordingly.
- If you want higher‑quality audio and integrated stereo DSP (crossover/eq), consider a small DSP/processor or a more advanced audio interface.
