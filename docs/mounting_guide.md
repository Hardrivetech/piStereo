Mounting Guide — Printing and using the faceplate template

1) Print the template
- Open `faceplate.svg` in a vector viewer (Inkscape/Illustrator) and ensure print scaling is 100% (no "fit to page").
- Print on A4 (or larger) paper. Verify the ruler marks on the print for correct scale (180 mm × 100 mm dashed box).

2) Verify scale
- Use a metal rule to confirm the dashed inner rectangle measures 180 mm × 100 mm exactly.

3) Transfer to bezel material
- Tape the print to the front bezel material (ABS/steel) and use a center punch for hole locations.
- For CNC/laser: export the SVG as DXF or the appropriate format for your cutter.

4) Drilling
- Use the recommended drill sizes: encoder hole 6.5 mm, power button 8.0 mm, panel buttons 12.0 mm.
- Deburr holes and test fit encoders/switches before finishing.

5) Cut the main opening
- Use a jigsaw, CNC, or laser cutter to cut the 180×100 opening. File and smooth edges.

6) Mount the internals
- Use nylon standoffs to mount the Pi to an internal bracket.
- Secure the USB audio interface and cable tie all wiring.

7) Rear connector plate
- Cut a rear rectangular plate sized to your connectors and label each jack.
- Use shielded RCA for preouts and secure speaker terminal blocks.

8) Final fit
- Test fit the bezel into the dash opening; adjust flange thickness if needed for snug fit.
